AI Governance & Evaluation

A controlled benchmark harness for testing the portfolio's agentic workflows against pre-defined fictional cases, expected assurance outcomes and evidence-quality checks.

What this demonstrates

The aim is not to claim perfect model accuracy. It shows how agentic workflows can be versioned, tested against controlled cases, measured for evidence traceability and reviewed when the expected and actual outcomes differ.
