Interview guide

What makes this agentic?

  1. The lead agent routes the case to relevant specialists.
  2. Evidence and technical reviewers challenge the complaint rather than accept allegations as facts.
  3. Customer impact informs a reasoned remedy proposal.
  4. Missing evidence sends the case back for investigation.
  5. A human owns the outcome, communication and redress authority.

In walkthrough mode the routing is scripted. Live mode uses the app’s AI service and lists the tools actually called.

The important boundary

A proposed impact payment and an agreement unwind are separate. Rejection, settlement, deposit refunds and use deductions need their own approved reconciliation.
